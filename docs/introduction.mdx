## What is FreightCode?

FreightCode is a UK customs SaaS that connects directly to HMRC's Customs Declaration Service (CDS) via official OAuth. It lets UK importers, customs brokers, and freight forwarders submit WCO-compliant declarations, track clearance status in real time, and maintain a full compliance audit trail.

  <Card title="HMRC CDS Submissions" icon="file-check">

## Who is it for?

- **UK importers** managing their own HMRC CDS filings
- **Customs brokers** filing on behalf of clients
- **Freight forwarders** needing real-time clearance visibility
