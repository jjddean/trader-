## Overview

FreightCode connects to HMRC CDS using the official OAuth 2.0 authorisation code flow. Your credentials are never stored — only the short-lived access token and refresh token are saved.

## Steps

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **Connect HMRC**
3. You will be redirected to the HMRC Government Gateway login
4. Sign in and grant FreightCode the `write:customs-declaration` scope
5. You are redirected back — your token is saved automatically

## Token Refresh

Tokens expire after 4 hours. FreightCode automatically refreshes them before expiry. If the refresh fails (e.g. you revoke access in Government Gateway), you will see a **Re-authenticate** prompt in the header.
