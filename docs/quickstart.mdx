---
title: Quickstart
description: Get up and running with FreightCode in minutes
---

## 1. Create your account

Sign up at [freightcode.co.uk](https://freightcode.co.uk) and verify your email.

## 2. Connect your HMRC account

Navigate to **Dashboard → Connect HMRC** and complete the OAuth flow using your Government Gateway credentials. FreightCode will securely store your access token — you only need to do this once.

<Note>
  You need an HMRC Developer Hub account and an active EORI number to connect.
</Note>

## 3. Create a declaration

Go to **Declarations → New Declaration**. Enter the origin country, HS code, and description of your goods.

## 4. Add goods items

Open the declaration and fill in the goods items tab:
- Commodity code (10 digits)
- Procedure code (DE 1/10)
- Origin country
- Gross and net weight
- Supporting documents (e.g. N853, Y930, Y929)

## 5. Submit to HMRC CDS

Click **Submit** from the declaration workspace. FreightCode validates the payload, maps it to WCO DEC-DMS:2 format, and posts it to HMRC CDS. You'll receive a conversation ID immediately and notifications as HMRC processes your declaration.
