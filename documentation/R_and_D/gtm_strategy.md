# 1. Executive Summary
The Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy for freightcode focuses on rapid adoption by targeting two specific, highly motivated demographics: SME Importers operating blindly under the new CDS regime, and Digital Freight Forwarders looking to automate data entry.

# 2. Target Segments

## 2.1 SME Importers
- **Pain Point:** They lack visibility into what brokers are filing on their behalf. They often overpay duties or incur penalties.
- **Value Proposition:** Connect your HMRC account once, and we'll visualize your history, find missed savings, and give you 1-click duty payments.
- **Acquisition Strategy:** Content marketing around "Missed Preference Savings," LinkedIn outreach, and free initial audits.

## 2.2 Digital Freight Forwarders & Customs Agents
- **Pain Point:** Manually re-typing commercial invoices into legacy customs software (e.g., Descartes, Sequoia) is slow and errors cost money.
- **Value Proposition:** Automatically extract data from PDFs with AI, and submit instantly to CDS via API.
- **Acquisition Strategy:** Direct B2B sales (Enterprise/Custom volume pricing), highlighting the hours saved per declaration.

# 3. Pricing & Packaging Structure

## 3.1 The "Free" Analytics Tier (Product-Led Growth Engine)
- **Features:** Historical data ingestion, Missed Savings Sandbox, Basic Compliance Scoring, UK Landed Cost Calculator.
- **Purpose:** Remove all friction to sign up. Get them to connect the HMRC API and see exactly how much money they left on the table.

## 3.2 The "Pro" Automation Tier (£99/mo)
- **Features:** Everything in Free + Smart Duty Pre-Fill (AI Matches), Direct HMRC CDS Submissions, Automated Transit Form Builder.
- **Purpose:** Once users trust the system and see the value, they upgrade to execute future clearances directly and instantly.

## 3.3 Enterprise Scale
- **Features:** Custom volume tiers, White-labeled customer portal, Dedicated Account Manager, API Access.
- **Purpose:** Capture large forwarding agencies who need unlimited volume.

# 4. Launch Phases
1. **Phase 1 (Closed Beta):** Onboard 10-20 SME Importers manually via the "Innovation Pathways" FCA partnership or direct network to test the Historical Sync and AI Invoice extraction.
2. **Phase 2 (Public Launch):** Open the "Free Analytics" tier to the public via organic search (SEO optimization around "CDS HMRC", "Landed Cost Calculators").
3. **Phase 3 (Monetization & API):** Launch the £99/mo tier once the CDS Submission API integration is rock-solid and Open Banking payments are live.
