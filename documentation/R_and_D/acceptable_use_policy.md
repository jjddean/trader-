# 1. Introduction
This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") outlines the rules and guidelines for using the freightcode® platform. By using the Service, you agree to comply with this Policy.

# 2. Prohibited Uses
You agree not to use, and not to encourage or allow any Third Party to use, the platform in the following prohibited ways:

## 2.1 Illegal or Fraudulent Activity
- Submitting fraudulent, deliberately inaccurate, or prohibited goods declarations to HM Revenue & Customs via our APIs.
- Using the platform for money laundering, terrorist financing, or evading legally required customs duties, tariffs, and VAT.
- Transmitting forged documents (e.g., manipulated commercial invoices or fake EUR.1 certificates) to the AI extraction engine to artificially lower duty rates.

## 2.2 System Abuse
- Attempting to bypass or break any security mechanism on the freightcode platform.
- Using automated scripts, bots, or scrapers to aggressively ping the freightcode API or the connected HMRC endpoints outside of reasonable usage limits, triggering rate-limiting blockades that affect other users.
- Reverse-engineering our AI classification models or extraction logic.

## 2.3 Harmful Content
- Uploading files (e.g., PDFs for extraction) containing viruses, trojans, worms, or any other malicious code.
- Processing highly sensitive personal data (e.g., health data or financial credit card information) through the invoice extraction engine, which is strictly built for commercial trade data.

# 3. Enforcement
freightcode reserves the right, but does not assume the obligation, to investigate any violation of this Policy. If we determine that a violation has occurred, we may, at our sole discretion:
1. Immediately suspend or terminate your account and API access.
2. Report suspected fraudulent or illegal customs activities directly to HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) and the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), providing all associated metadata and logs as required by UK law.

# 4. Reporting Violations
If you become aware of any violation of this Policy, you must immediately notify us at `security@freightcode.com`.
