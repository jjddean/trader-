# freightcode®: The Core Product Vision & Pitch

## The Problem: The £570m Data Gap
HMRC recently invested **£570 million** into the new Single Customs Platform (Customs Declaration Service - CDS), modernizing the UK's border infrastructure and creating a centralized database of every import and export. 

They also launched a self-service system ("Get Customs Data") allowing businesses to access their own records. However, **only 1% of businesses currently sign up to access their customs data**. 

Why? Because government interfaces are notoriously clunky, technical, and difficult for non-specialists to navigate. The data exists, but the usability does not.

## Our Solution: The Accessibility Layer
**freightcode®** bridges this massive awareness and adoption gap. We provide the missing, modern "data accessibility layer" that HMRC failed to build. 

By connecting directly to HMRC's API infrastructure, freightcode pulls complex customs data into a beautiful, intuitive dashboard. We democratize customs data for the 99% of businesses who are currently flying blind, empowering freight forwarders and importers to track their trading activity, manage compliance, and conduct audits seamlessly.

## The "Killer Feature": 1-Click Customs Payments
Solving data access is only half the battle; execution is the other. Every accepted customs declaration generates an immediate HMRC bill for Import Duty and Import VAT. 

Currently, settling these bills involves manual bank transfers or navigating separate government portals using specifically generated reference numbers (e.g., CDSI references).

freightcode eliminates this friction by integrating **Open Banking APIs directly into the dashboard**.

### The Payments Value Proposition:
1. **Immediate Payments (Cash Account):** When HMRC returns the calculated tax bill, users can click a single button within freightcode to instantly settle the Import Duty and VAT via Open Banking, ensuring their goods are released from the port without delay.
2. **Duty Deferment Account (DDA) Management:** For businesses using a DDA to pay monthly via direct debit, freightcode can monitor their HMRC credit limits. If their limit is running low—risking blocked shipments—the app can alert them and allow instant top-ups via Open Banking to keep their supply chain moving.

## Summary
freightcode is not just a filing tool; it is the comprehensive operating system for UK customs. We unlock the £570m HMRC data vault for the masses and close the loop with frictionless, integrated Open Banking payments.
